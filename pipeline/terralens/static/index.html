A LITTLE GUIDANCE

Start with a question.

01

Ask about a place.

Name a college, landmark or city anywhere. Similar names may return several possible matches; inspect their addresses before choosing.

02

Describe what you need.

For geographic patterns, include the object, location and a distance, such as “buildings within 300 metres of a river in Pune.” One search box handles places, geographic records and available imagery.

03

Inspect the evidence.

Select a result to see its coordinates, source and available checks. Visual discoveries require suitable imagery. If the area is not covered, we will tell you. A satellite basemap alone does not provide AI evidence.

04

Take your findings with you.

Download GeoJSON with the returned geometries and their sources. Place records, image scenes and model observations have distinct evidence; partial coverage remains visible.
